# 09 · Economy — Loot Value, Fencing, the Greed Curve & Money Sinks

> How loot becomes power. Couples the stealing loop (07) to the memory engine (05) via traceability,
> and feeds meta-progression (02). All choices locked round 12.

## Contextual valuation (locked)

An item's realised worth is dynamic, not a tag:

    value ≈ base × condition × buyer_fit × coolness × (1 / market_saturation)

- **base / condition** — what it is and its state.
- **buyer_fit** — the right specialist fence pays far more (a signet to a documents fence ≪ to the
  right buyer).
- **coolness** — how far a *hot* (recently-stolen, described) item has cooled (see fencing).
- **market saturation** — flooding a fence/market with similar goods depresses the price.

So *when*, *to whom*, and *in what condition* you sell are all real decisions.

## Fencing — specialist fences + hot→cool laundering (locked)

- **Specialist fences:** jewels, art, documents, general — each with **capital limits** (can't buy
  your whole haul at once), prices, **risk**, and a **relationship** with you that grows or sours.
  Fences are open **by day** (04) and **remember faces** — dealing repeatedly builds *personal
  recognition* (05b), a debt as much as an asset.
- **Hot → cool:** a described unique piece is **evidence** (05). Fencing it **too soon, or to the
  wrong buyer, points back at you** and can **reopen a Case** (a fence gossips; an investigator
  traces the sale). Items **cool over ticks**; laundering means *waiting* or **breaking them down**
  (pry the gem from its recognizable setting) to shed the description at a value cost.
- This is the economic core of module 10's **fencing-network** system. **v1 scope line (review
  pass):** everything in this module ships in v1 (module 12); the module-10 deep-dive adds the
  *depth* layer on top — relationship arcs, favours, fence-given jobs, rivalries between fences.

## The greed curve — escalating risk, diminishing safety (locked)

The run's central tension, made numeric:

- **Cumulative heat rises** and the **town hardens** (routines/patrols react, 04) the longer you
  stay and the more you take.
- **Fresh loot gets riskier to move & fence** as heat climbs and markets saturate.
- **Big scores spike both** payout and danger.
- ⇒ **Leaving is a genuine judgement call**, and greed is *occasionally fatal.* Curve constants are
  **tunable** (feel-test, like day length & carry). This is the numeric spine of module 02's
  open-ended run.

## Money sinks — growth AND survival (locked)

Wealth flows to both horizons, so you're always choosing bank-for-later vs. spend-to-survive-now:

- **Meta-progression (between runs):** tool unlocks, lair upgrades (02) — permanent power, and the
  host for unfolding systems (10).
- **In-run:** consumables & tools, disguises, **bribes**, **information/tips**, and **buying off heat
  or a Case** (05c) — immediate survival and problem-solving.

## Determinism

Prices, coolness timers, saturation, and buyer behaviour are **deterministic seeded** functions;
"cooling" is measured in **sim ticks/days**, never wall-clock. Replaying a trace reproduces the same
prices and sales.
