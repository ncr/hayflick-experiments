# 02 · Run Structure, Session Flow & Meta-Progression

## The run (locked — round 2)

- **Horizon — open-ended until too hot.** A run has no fixed length. You stay in one town as
  long as you dare. The town's hunt escalates *continuously* as evidence accumulates — there
  is no reset and no clock forcing you out. Leaving town (extraction) is always available and
  is the player's judgement call. **The core tension is greed vs. heat.**
- **Objective — layered.** Two intertwined tracks run at once:
  - *Survive & build* — freeform theft for money and supplies that keeps you going and funds
    meta-progression. Valid to spend a whole run doing only this.
  - *Big scores (opt-in)* — named high-value targets (an heirloom, a vault, a noble's seal)
    you choose to attempt for large risk/reward. Declining is fine; chasing them is where runs
    peak.
- **Getting caught — lose loot, spike heat, play on.** Being caught red-handed *or*
  identified-and-cornered does **not** end the run by default. You're searched (un-fenced loot
  confiscated), your identity/heat spikes hard, and you're released or you break away. The run
  continues, now much harder. **Being hunted is content, not a game-over screen.** (Review-pass
  fix: the only genuine hard end is **death** — locked in 05c's failure floor, round 7. Captures
  never end the run; they escalate a punishment ladder instead. "Heat saturation" as an end-state
  was considered and rejected.)

## Extraction & the greed curve

Leaving town **banks** the run's fenced gains into meta-progression. The longer you stay, the
more you can take and the hotter it gets. The design job (module 09) is a payoff curve that
keeps "one more night" always tempting and occasionally fatal.

## Meta-progression — what persists across runs (locked — round 2)

Towns are disposable; **the thief is not.** Three persistent tracks, all in play:

1. **Skills & tools.** Skills grow **by use** (learn-by-doing — refined in round 9, module 07);
   gear unlocks *verbs* — lockpicks, blackjack, chloroform, disguises, climbing kit. Growth
   changes *what you can do and attempt*, never a bare to-hit number.
2. **A home base you build — TWO places (review-pass resolution).**
   - The **persistent lair** sits *outside the towns*: the between-run meta hub where extracted
     wealth becomes upgrades (workshop, tool fabricators, a forger's bench when unlocked, trophy
     wall). It is the natural *host* for newly-unlocked systems (see below), and the lone-wolf
     constraint (module 10) applies: it hosts *tools and contacts, not a crew of members.*
   - In each town you establish a temporary **safehouse** (rent a room, squat): the run's stash,
     wardrobe storage (06), sleeping/time-skip point (04), and planning board (11). **Lair
     upgrades improve what your safehouses can do.** Towns stay disposable; the lair does not.
3. **Notoriety as a legend.** Reputation grows into a named ghost-thief. Towns start warier,
   bounties climb, songs are sung. **Fame is a cost as much as a reward**, and it sits in
   deliberate tension with per-run anonymity: the *legend* travels between towns even though
   the *face* does not — until a town connects a face to the legend.

## Design philosophy — the game unfolds (locked — round 2)

The core stealing loop is the **foundation, not the ceiling.** The game keeps introducing
*new systems* across its whole length; the player should repeatedly find it is bigger than
they thought. Meta-progression is the delivery vehicle — new systems arrive as you grow. The
evolving catalogue of these system-layers is its own living module (**10**); round 3 maps the
horizon and how systems unlock.
