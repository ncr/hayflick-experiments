# 10 · The Systems Catalogue — the unfolding layers

> Living list of the system-layers the game unfolds *beyond* the core stealing loop.
> Selected in round 3. This module maps the **ambition skyline**; it is NOT a build
> commitment — the core (stealing + suspicion) must be excellent before any of these land.
> Each system gets its own deep module when it is promoted to active design.

## Framing constraints (locked — round 3)

- **Lone wolf.** The player operates ALONE. No recruited crew, no guild-of-members, no
  rival-gang faction layer to manage. Your "organization" is tools, contacts, reputation, and
  a lair (02) — never a roster of companions. (The whole crew/guild/rival cluster was explicitly
  cut.)
- **Systems arrive by a MIX of routes** — some gated by notoriety, some built into the lair
  with wealth, some discovered in the world. Each system's unlock should match its fiction.
- **The core is the floor.** Stealing + the suspicion/deduction engine is the foundation every
  other system plugs into.

## Tier 0 — the foundation (always present)

- Core stealing loop — verbs, loot, stealth — module 07.
- **Suspicion, memory & deduction engine** — module 05. The hub every social system writes to.
- Identity & disguise — module 06.

## The underworld economy (all four selected)

- **Fencing network.** Individual fences, each specializing (jewels, art, documents). Hot goods
  must *cool* before safe sale; distinctive items are traceable back to you if fenced too fast
  or to the wrong buyer. → couples to memory: a stolen item is itself *evidence*.
- **Forgery & counterfeiting.** Fake seals, papers, coins, badges. Feeds impersonation (identity
  papers) and lets you fabricate or launder evidence. → couples to the deduction engine.
- **Smuggling & black market.** Move contraband between districts (and between towns across
  runs); buy-low/sell-high across legality gradients; run goods past checkpoints.
- **Run your own racket.** Flip to crime-boss: protection, loan-sharking, fencing for others.
  Passive income that becomes an *enterprise the law can investigate and dismantle.* Keep it
  lone-wolf — run via coerced/bribed contacts, not a managed gang.

## Social & information warfare (all four selected — the deduction-engine attack surface)

These do not sit *beside* the suspicion engine; they **write into it**. Module 05 must be built
as something the player can attack, not merely evade.

- **Impersonation.** Become a *specific* person (disguise + papers + their routine) and walk in
  the front door. The deepest form of "appearance is identity" — feed the deduction engine a
  false but internally-consistent answer.
- **Framing & misdirection.** Plant false descriptions, drop another's belongings at the scene,
  seed rumors. Redirect the town's case onto someone else.
- **Blackmail & leverage.** Learn secrets (theft, eavesdropping, surveillance) and coerce NPCs —
  to look away, unlock a door, testify falsely, feed intel. Information as weapon and currency.
- **Bribery & corruption.** Buy guards, officials, witnesses, fences. Money converts heat into
  safety — but bought loyalty is fragile and a corrupt official is a liability who can be leaned
  on or exposed.
- _Note:_ one-off **intel purchases** (buy a tip — who's carrying what, patrol changes) live here
  under bribery/leverage as transactions, NOT as a managed informant network (cut with the crew
  cluster).

## Unlock routing (locked — round 3; refine per system later)

Mix of **notoriety tiers · home-base investment · world discovery.** Each system slots to
whichever fits its fiction — e.g. forgery = discover a forger who teaches you; racket =
notoriety-gated; a planning/war-room capability = home-base investment.
