# 06 · Player, Identity & Disguise

> The proactive counterpart to the recognition engine (05a/05b): the tools you use to control what
> the town can perceive and remember about your look. All choices locked round 8.
> **Scope note:** this attacks *description-heat* (05b layer 1). It does NOT beat *personal
> recognition* (05b layer 2) — the deliberate limit that keeps disguise from being a cure-all.

## The player character (anonymous)

- No fixed name or face the world tracks (round 1). "You" is: a set of **hard traits** + your
  **current worn layers** + any **permanent marks** acquired via punishment (05c).
- **Hard traits** (build, height, gait) are difficult/impossible to change and are what **personal
  recognition** binds to. **Layers** are freely changed. **Marks** (brand, cropped ear, limp) are
  permanent additions to your describable features — hideable, not removable.

## Appearance = layered slots over hard traits (locked)

- **Slots:** headwear/hood · face/mask · torso/coat · legs · footwear · accessories. Each worn item
  contributes **features** (colors, silhouette, concealment) to the live `AppearanceDescription`
  the perception engine reads.
- **Concealment:** a hood hides hair/head, a mask hides face, a cloak blurs build. Hidden features
  read as `Unknown` to observers ⇒ vaguer descriptions ⇒ weaker matches.
- **Hard traits & marks** sit under the layers: still potentially observable (a keen witness notes
  your build or limp even hooded) and the anchor for personal recognition.
- Disguise = **changing which layers you wear** to stop matching a live wanted profile.

## Wardrobe — acquire, carry limited, change in cover (locked)

- **Acquire** garments by stealing, buying, or looting (a uniform off an unconscious guard; a cloak
  from a washing line; tailored pieces from a shop).
- **Carry a limited set** — outfits take inventory space; you can't haul the whole wardrobe. The
  **safehouse** stores the rest.
- **Change only when unobserved** (an alley, a dark room, the safehouse). **Changing in view is a
  suspicious, witnessable act** — "I saw them shed the green hood by the well" is a powerful
  `Observation` that links your two looks and can defeat the whole point.

## Contextual blending — social roles & access (locked)

- Every outfit carries a **social role/class**: guard, servant, noble, laborer, clergy, beggar,
  tradesman…
- **Right context** (dressed to belong where you are) **lowers baseline suspicion** and can **grant
  access** — a guard's uniform opens the barracks, a servant's smock the kitchens, clergy the chapel.
- **Wrong context raises suspicion** — a beggar in the noble quarter, or a "guard" nobody recognizes
  on a small watch, draws eyes. Belonging is role *and* place *and* being known.
- This is the **on-ramp to impersonation** (module 10): role-dressing is generic blending;
  impersonation is becoming a *specific* person on top of it.

## Disguise tells & integrity — pierceable, not perfect (locked)

A disguise buys **distance and doubt, not invincibility.** It can be pierced by:

- **Close inspection** — a suspicious guard at conversation range resolves more features.
- **Wrong behavior / missing knowledge** — a "servant" who doesn't know the house; a "guard" off
  the patrol pattern.
- **Physical flaws** — a bloodstain on stolen clothes, an ill fit, gear that doesn't match the role.
- **Being seen donning it** (see wardrobe).
- **Personal recognition** (05b) — someone who knows the *real you* under the costume, or the
  impersonated person's **close colleagues**, who aren't fooled by the uniform.

Piercing a disguise mid-scene is a classic unraveling moment; maintaining a cover is active play.

## Interplay with the crown jewel (the deliberate limit)

- Disguise **defeats description-heat**: change your look, stop matching the profile, exposure
  collapses (05b layer 1). The everyday loop.
- Disguise **does NOT defeat personal recognition** (05b layer 2) or **piercing tells**. The fence
  who's dealt with you, the guard who searched you, the brand under your hood — those need the
  *other* tools (avoid, bribe, blackmail, discredit, eliminate; 05c). Keeping these layers distinct
  is what stops disguise from trivializing the game.

## Determinism

Feature contribution, concealment, blending, and tell-resolution are deterministic functions of worn
state + observer context at a tick; any variance (does *this* guard notice the ill fit) is seeded.
